# Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL)
## Electricity Tariff Schedule — General Purpose Category 2 (GP-2)
**Effective Date: July 1, 2024**

### Clause 4.1: Peak Energy Charges
Under General Purpose Tariff 2 (GP-2) and Industrial Schedule I-2, electricity consumption during the designated peak window (18:00 to 22:30 hours daily) shall be billed at the high-demand unit rate of LKR 58.00 per kilowatt-hour (kWh). 
Commercial institutions, university campuses, and institutional facilities are strongly incentivized to minimize grid draw during this four-and-a-half hour interval through load-shifting, chiller precooling, or battery energy storage system (BESS) peak-shaving dispatch.

### Clause 4.2: Day and Off-Peak Energy Charges
Standard day-time electricity consumption occurring between 05:30 and 18:00 hours shall be billed at the standard rate of LKR 30.00 per kWh. 
Off-peak energy consumption occurring during night-time hours between 22:30 and 05:30 hours shall be billed at the subsidized rate of LKR 15.00 per kWh. 
Facility energy storage assets should schedule charging cycles predominantly within the off-peak window to capitalize on tariff arbitrage.

### Clause 6.3: Maximum Demand Surcharge and Penalty
A monthly maximum demand charge of LKR 1,100.00 per kVA is levied on the single highest 15-minute integrated demand recorded by the utility tri-vector meter during the billing cycle. 
Institutions exceeding their sanctioned contract demand threshold shall incur an additional 20% punitive surcharge on the excess demand. Sudden coincidental load spikes, including simultaneous startup of centralized chiller compressors or high-power laboratory machinery, must be strictly avoided or mitigated via automated load-limiting controls.

### Clause 7.1: Behind-the-Meter Solar Photovoltaic Net Metering
Institutions operating behind-the-meter rooftop solar PV systems under Net Metering or Net Accounting agreements shall offset energy consumption on a half-hourly net interval basis. Exported solar power during daylight hours does not offset the Maximum Demand charge, reinforcing the operational requirement for local battery storage peak shaving.
