# ASHRAE Standard 55-2023
## Thermal Environmental Conditions for Human Occupancy
**Application: University Classrooms, Lecture Theatres, and Computing Facilities**

### Section 5.3: Indoor Operative Temperature Comfort Envelopes
For educational spaces including lecture halls, tutorial rooms, and computing laboratories characterized by predominantly sedentary activity (1.0 to 1.2 met metabolic rate) and standard indoor seasonal attire (0.5 to 1.0 clo), the acceptable operative temperature envelope ranges between 21.0°C and 25.5°C. 
Operating HVAC cooling systems to maintain indoor air within this envelope ensures a Predicted Mean Vote (PMV) between -0.5 and +0.5, satisfying the 80% indoor comfort acceptability criteria.

### Section 6.2: Precooling and Thermal Inertia Guidelines
To achieve peak electrical demand shaving without compromising student comfort, active precooling of the building thermal mass is permitted prior to high-occupancy intervals. 
However, precooling setpoints must not depress the indoor operative temperature below the 21.0°C lower boundary. Furthermore, the maximum rate of operative temperature drift during load-shedding periods shall not exceed 1.1°C per hour to avoid localized thermal discomfort.

### Section 7.1: Air Velocity and Humidity Constraints
Within air-conditioned lecture spaces, mean air velocity should remain between 0.15 m/s and 0.25 m/s to prevent draft sensations. Relative humidity must be maintained between 40% and 65% to avoid microbial growth and respiratory irritation.
