### GP-2 Table: Applicability and time-of-use energy rates
GP-2 applies to each individual point of supply delivered and metered at 400/230 V nominal where the contract demand exceeds 42 kVA. Peak is 18:30 to 22:30 at LKR 26.60 per kWh. Day-time consumption between 05:30 and 18:30 is billed at LKR 21.80 per kWh. Off-peak is 22:30 to 05:30 at LKR 15.40 per kWh. The monthly fixed charge is LKR 3,000.

### GP-2 Table: Maximum demand charge
The maximum demand charge for GP-2 is LKR 1,100 per kVA per month.
